Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the information needed to provide Habitly's features:

  • Account Information: When you sign up with email, we collect your name, email address, and password (passwords are handled securely by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us). When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account.
  • Email Verification Data: To verify your email address or reset your password, we generate a one-time code (OTP) that is temporarily stored and sent to your email address.
  • Profile Details: Any profile information you choose to update, including an optional profile photo you take with your camera or select from your gallery.
  • Habit & Activity Data: Habit titles, icons, categories, goals you choose during personalization, targets, durations, schedules, reminder times, favorites, completion history, streaks, and focus timer sessions.
  • On-Device Preferences: App settings such as your last selected focus timer duration and onboarding status are stored locally on your device.

Habitly does not collect your precise location, contacts, health records, or payment information, and it does not show third-party advertisements.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • Securely save and synchronize your habits and progress to your account.
  • Schedule the habit reminders and alarms you set on your device.
  • Calculate streaks, completion rates, progress rings, and other insights.
  • Send verification and password reset codes to your email address.
  • Respond to support requests, troubleshoot issues, and improve the app.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell your personal information. Limited data is processed by trusted service providers solely to operate Habitly:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage) — account sign-in, habit data storage and sync, and profile photo storage.
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in with your Google account.
  • Transactional email providers (such as Resend or Gmail SMTP) — delivering verification and password reset codes to your email.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security commitments. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and services.

4. Device Permissions

Habitly may request the following permissions. You can manage them anytime in your device settings:

  • Notifications & Exact Alarms: To deliver habit reminders and alarms at the times you choose. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Camera & Photos: Only when you choose to take or upload a profile photo.
  • Run at Startup & Vibration: To restore your scheduled reminders after a device restart and to vibrate when an alarm rings.
  • Internet: To sign in and sync your data.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers and transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your data is restricted to your authenticated account by security rules. While we use industry-standard safeguards, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

We keep your account and habit data for as long as your account is active. One-time verification codes are temporary and expire shortly after they are issued. When you delete your account, your data is removed as described below.

7. Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your Habitly account and all associated data at any time:

  • In the app: Open Habitly and go to Profile > Delete Account, then confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes your authentication record, all your habits and progress, your profile document, and your profile photo from our servers.
  • By email: If you can no longer access the app, email info@appstick.com.bd with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from the email address linked to your account. We will verify your request and permanently delete your account and data within 30 days.
Deleted data cannot be recovered. Data stored only on your device (such as local preferences) is removed when you uninstall the app.

8. Your Choices & Rights

  • View and update your profile information inside the app.
  • Edit or delete individual habits at any time.
  • Turn reminders and notifications on or off.
  • Request a copy, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

9. Children's Privacy

Habitly is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at info@appstick.com.bd and we will promptly delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Significant changes may also be announced in the app. Continued use of Habitly after an update means you accept the revised policy.
