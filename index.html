Privacy First

Privacy Policy

Habitly respects your privacy and is fully committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you register or sign in using Email, Google, or Apple, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo.

Habit & Productivity Data: Habit titles, frequencies, daily targets, completion history, streaks, focus timer logs, and user preferences stored to deliver core app functionality.

Device & Push Notification Info: Device model, OS version, and notification permission tokens to deliver habit alarms and reminder notifications.

Advertising & Diagnostics: When advertisements are enabled, third-party advertising partners such as Google AdMob may collect and process device advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / IDFA) and app interaction data to serve non-intrusive or relevant ads.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly to authenticate your account, securely synchronize your habits across devices, send requested reminders on time, calculate progress insights, troubleshoot issues, and protect account security.

3. Third-Party Services

We never sell your personal information. We partner only with trusted infrastructure and advertising providers operating under strict privacy safeguards:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage for secure account management and cloud sync.
  • Google Sign-In & Apple Sign-In: For seamless authentication.
  • Google AdMob: For serving mobile advertisements in compliance with Google Developer Policies.

4. Account & Data Deletion (Your Rights)

We give users full control over their personal data. You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account immediately by opening the Habitly app, navigating to Profile > Delete Account, and confirming the prompt. This permanently erases your authentication record, habits, and profile images from our Firebase servers.
  • Web / Email Deletion Request: You can submit a deletion request by emailing us at info@appstick.com.bd with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Your account and all associated data will be permanently purged within 30 days of verification.

5. Children's Privacy

Habitly does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at info@appstick.com.bd and we will promptly delete it.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact Appstick at info@appstick.com.bd or visit us at 50, KDA Outer Bypass Rd, Khulna 9100, Bangladesh.
