Features

Everything You Need to Build Lasting Habits

Designed with intention to help you overcome friction, organize daily routines, and stay consistent effortlessly.

Custom Habit Routines

Create habits with customized durations, target pages, daily frequencies, and flexible schedules tailored to your lifestyle.

Integrated Pomodoro Timer

Boost your productivity with focus sessions linked to your habits, quick presets (15m, 25m, 30m, 45m, 60m), and short 5-minute breaks.

Streak & Growth Analytics

Stay motivated with streak counters, completion percentages, weekly growth graphs, and monthly consistency heatmaps.
